# Everglen Phase 2 Complete

Phase 2, **Society & Civilization**, is now complete.

## Civilization layer

- Social classes: peasants, artisans, merchants, scholars, clergy, soldiers, nobility and royalty.
- Institutions: guilds, councils, courts, military orders, academies and faith institutions.
- Governance: councils, legitimacy, cohesion, institutional power and competing political movements.
- Economy: markets, prices, production, prosperity, class needs and class grievances.
- Class conflict: organized pressure, open conflict, strikes and concessions.
- Social unrest: protests, uprisings, rebellion risk and migration pressure.
- Regime crisis: factional political pressure, claimants and civil-war risk.
- Civil war: real rebel factions, territorial footholds, sieges, settlement capture, counter-capture and victory outcomes.

## Long-term civilization evolution

- Settlement literacy and education.
- Knowledge accumulation through academies, teachers and existing medieval technology.
- Dominant culture and belief identity.
- Cultural diversity and religious tolerance/tension.
- Emergent law selection between customary, royal, feudal and mercantile traditions.
- Civilization eras from Early Kingdom through High Medieval Flourishing.
- Persistent civilization history across generations.

## Diplomacy

Kingdoms now develop persistent bilateral relations from culture, faith, prosperity, trade, borders and war history.

Diplomatic states include:

- Alliances.
- Trade pacts.
- Truces.
- Trust and hostility.
- Diplomatic history.

Diplomacy influences stability, prosperity and international power while leaving actual war resolution to the authoritative war system.

## Persistence

Civilization identity, education, diplomacy, treaties and related Phase 2 state are ordinary simulation data and are retained by the existing v3 world serializer.

## Architecture

Phase 2 systems remain state-only and use the existing simulation scheduler. They do not create world-canvas listeners or independent render loops.
