# Everglen Asset Integration Pass

Selected assets supplied by the project owner and curated into runtime atlases.

- Pixel Crawler / Anokolisa: NPCs, character animation source, weapons/tools and building components.
- Overworld Biomes Free Starter: overworld terrain, biome transitions and nature source assets.
- Roguey Pixel Pack Arms & Armor: armour/equipment reference sheets supplied for integration.
- CraftPix Knight sprites: knight/combat sprites, used for military visual class.
- CraftPix farm animals and hunting animals: farm and wildlife sprites.
- Pixel1992 Sunny Castles: castle landmark assets.
- Medieval Town & Fantasy 2D Mega Props Pack: settlement, market, tavern, blacksmith and village props.
- Boats/Vessels: boat, raft and ship assets, with the large ship currently represented as a warship.

Runtime atlases are derived/curated project resources. The original packs remain governed by their supplied licenses and are not redistributed as standalone asset packs.
